## Architectural Interior Methodology

At SAAR Business Support Solution, interior design is not superficial decoration. Every layout, surface transition, and illumination zone is calibrated to improve the experiential quality of the built space.

* **Material Honesty:** We prioritize authentic materials—honed stone, natural wood veneers, architectural brass, and lime plaster.
* **Spatial Efficiency:** Built-in joinery is engineered to maximize usable square footage while eliminating visual clutter.
