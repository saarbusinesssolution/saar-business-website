## Spatial Concept Narrative

The Courtyard Pavilion serves as an architectural study in restrained luxury. By removing non-structural partition walls and orienting primary living functions around an interior lightwell, the concept demonstrates how residential architecture can achieve emotional tranquility through material restraint.
