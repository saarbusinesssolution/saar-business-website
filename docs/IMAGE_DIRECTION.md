# SAAR Architectural Image Direction & Art Specification

## 1. Visual Mission & Brand Alignment

Imagery across the **Saar Business Support Solution** platform is not decorative filler. It serves as visual proof of spatial intelligence, construction rigor, and architectural restraint.

Every photograph or conceptual visualization must visually reinforce the brand philosophy:  
> **“Designed with Purpose. Executed with Precision.”**

### Brand Palette Resonance
* **Deep Navy (`#173A5E`):** Appears organically through deep architectural shadows, steel fenestrations, exterior dusk skyframes, and high-contrast furniture accents. Never forced as arbitrary painted navy walls.
* **Architectural Gold (`#B99052`):** Appears authentically in brushed brass hardware, warm recessed 2700K lighting washes, custom joinery handles, and refined metal inlays. Never gaudy or metallic gold leaf.
* **Charcoal (`#202020`):** Present in powder-coated industrial partition frames, dark slate/granite accents, and structured architectural borders.
* **Soft White (`#F4F1EA`):** Reflected in lime-wash plaster walls, natural honed travertine floors, warm linen drapery, and soft daylight gradients.

---

## 2. Architectural Photographic Standards

### 2.1 Spatial Authenticity
* **Plausible Geometry:** Rooms must exhibit believable Indian urban residential and commercial scales (e.g. 10–12 ft ceiling heights, realistic structural column spans, true-to-life door clearances).
* **True Verticals:** Two-point perspective architectural framing with zero vertical lens distortion. Vertical wall corners and door jambs must remain strictly parallel to the frame edges.
* **Human-Scale Furniture:** Seating, tables, and cabinetry must maintain realistic ergonomic proportions relative to floor plates.

### 2.2 Material Honesty & Tactility
* Surfaces must exhibit tangible physical texture: subtle veining in silver travertine, natural open grain in walnut/teak millwork, soft matte sheen on lime plaster, and authentic woven texture in linen textiles.
* Avoid plasticized, hyper-glossy, or synthetic AI "sheen" across wooden and stone elements.

### 2.3 Lighting Calibration
* **Natural Daylight:** Scenes are anchored by generous diffused natural morning or soft late-afternoon daylight pouring through floor-to-ceiling glazing or internal courtyards.
* **Artificial Integration:** Supplementary architectural lighting is restrained: warm indirect cove washes (2700K–3000K), discrete linear ceiling slots, and minimalist task fixtures. Overexposed HDR glare is strictly prohibited.

---

## 3. Strict Anti-Patterns & Exclusions

> [!CAUTION]
> The following visual tropes degrade architectural credibility and are strictly forbidden:
> * ❌ **Fictional "Palatial" Grandeur:** Oversized hotel-atrium rooms masquerading as realistic private apartments.
> * ❌ **Excessive Marble & Gold:** Heavy neo-classical or faux-baroque gold mouldings and shiny polished mirror-finish floors.
> * ❌ **Impossible Architecture:** Staircases leading into walls, floating structural elements, distorted window grids.
> * ❌ **Fake Signage & Watermarks:** Embedded logos, synthetic brand names on walls, or watermarks.
> * ❌ **Overprocessed HDR:** Artificial neon-like oversaturation or flattened tonal dynamics.

---

## 4. Concept Disclosure & Public Integrity

* Every AI-generated visualization or conceptual render is watermarked with an automatic **"Design Concept"** badge on project cards and editorial placements.
* Concept imagery is explicitly labeled as illustrative in meta descriptions and captions, ensuring prospective clients understand it represents SAAR's design and spatial capabilities rather than commissioned past builds.
